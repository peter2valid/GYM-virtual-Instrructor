1. 🎯 PRODUCT DEFINITION
What you are building

A multi-tenant SaaS platform for gyms that provides:

QR-based access to workouts
Virtual guided workout sessions
Member tracking (attendance + activity)
Gym analytics dashboard
Optional branding (premium gyms)
Core idea (simple)
Scan QR → Enter gym → Train → Track → Analyze
Business model
B2B SaaS (you sell to gyms)
Monthly subscription (KES)
3 tiers:
Starter (no tracking)
Track (login + analytics)
Premium (branding + advanced features)

Definition

 └── Gym C (tenant_id = C)

Why
